# PRODUCT.md — hirokuwana.com

register: brand

## Product purpose

The personal site of Hiro Kuwana: solo founder of Kaiwa, a real-time AI
conversation platform for language learners. The site is a portfolio and a
notebook, not a store. It must read as the home of one focused builder.

## Users

1. **Investors** who check whether Hiro is serious and undistracted. They must
   read: full-time on Kaiwa, a record of shipped cycles, no side hustle.
2. **AI researchers and engineering peers** (language AI, voice AI). They must
   read: this person ships production systems alone, and thinks clearly.
3. **Educators and readers** who follow the essays and AI guides.

## Brand and tone

- Voice inspirations (from PERSONAL_BRAND_STRATEGY.md): Paul Graham essays,
  Feynman playfulness, Collison-style craft and decade-thinking.
- Bilingual English / Japanese. Japanese is a first-class locale, not a
  translation afterthought.
- Aesthetic: washi paper, ink, moss and vermilion (shu) accents, kanji seals,
  vertical text marks, ledger-like mono details. Calm, zen, unhurried.
- Hard authorship rule: sentences attributed to Hiro are written by Hiro.
  Claude supplies structure and functional labels only. See COPY_WORKSHEET.md.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing-page energy: gradient heroes, metric cards, CTA buttons
  everywhere.
- "Available for hire" freelancer portfolios. The site must never signal
  availability.
- AI-flavored prose. If a sentence could come from a template, it does not
  ship.

## Strategic principles

- Evidence over adjectives: numbers, dates, shipped things.
- Sunset projects are completed cycles with lessons, never failures to hide.
- Slow web: few sections, generous whitespace, no engagement mechanics.
