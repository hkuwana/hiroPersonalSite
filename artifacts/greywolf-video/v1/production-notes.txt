Editorial choices
- The numerical example is explicitly illustrative and uses dummy data.
  Two values, 125 and 55, sum to 180. A draft total of 182 is flagged.
  No client result, time-saving metric or real financial data is implied.
- Replaced "check every output" with "test outputs with code, and flag
  exceptions for review" to describe a check without promising infallibility.
- Omitted "Nothing you type is stored." No brief form or its storage behavior
  was available to verify that statement.
- Dates, names, build-ahead commitment and async deliverables come from the
  supplied draft. They are not independently verified calendar facts.
- No live brief URL was supplied. The call to action says "Open the brief link
  in your invitation" so the draft can be reviewed without inventing a URL.
- Names are used only in this requested Greywolf review asset. The public site
  was not edited. No email, upload, publishing or sharing action was performed.
