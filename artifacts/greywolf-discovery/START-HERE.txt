REBUILD
Python: see dependencies.json (Pillow, numpy, fontTools).
ffmpeg/ffprobe must be on PATH. The supplied TTFs carry their license notices.
1. python3 render.py
2. ffmpeg -i visual-silent.mp4 -i review-sound.wav -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0
   -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 160k -t 80 -movflags +faststart
   otterd-greywolf-discovery-review.mp4
3. ffmpeg -i otterd-greywolf-discovery-review.mp4 -vf "setpts=0.5*PTS"
   -af atempo=2.0 -r 30 -c:v libx264 -crf 19 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac
   -t 40 -movflags +faststart otterd-greywolf-discovery-review-2x.mp4
4. python3 build_page.py
